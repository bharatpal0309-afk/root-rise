Last updated: 20 October 2026

Root & Rise is a personal tree plantation and monitoring journal. These terms explain the intended use of this version and your responsibilities when keeping records.

1. Using the application

Use Root & Rise to register trees, record observations, manage care tasks, and export or import your records. Use the application responsibly and only for lawful activities. It does not provide permission to access land, plant trees, or carry out work on someone else's property.

2. Your records and photos

You remain responsible for the information and photos you add, including their accuracy and your permission to use them. Only include other people's names, images, or location details when you have appropriate permission. Adding content does not transfer ownership of it to the application.

3. Browser storage and backups

This version stores tree records and photos in your browser using IndexedDB. It does not provide an account, cloud backup, or automatic syncing between devices. Records can be lost if browser data is cleared, the browser removes stored data, or your device is lost or damaged.

Export backups regularly and store them securely. Backup files contain your entered information and photos. Anyone with access to the browser profile or exported files may be able to view that information. Importing a backup adds new record IDs and skips IDs already present; it does not replace existing records.

4. Health records and statistics

Health statuses, measurements, and dates are entered by users. Dashboard totals are calculated from those records and are not independently verified. Recorded survival is the number of trees marked Healthy or Needs attention divided by all assessed trees. Trees marked Not assessed are excluded; no survival percentage is displayed when no trees have been assessed.

The application does not diagnose disease, monitor trees through sensors, or certify environmental benefits. Use appropriate professional advice when a tree's condition or work around it requires specialist assessment.

5. Care schedules

Care dates appear in the application when you open it. This version does not send background, email, or push reminders. You are responsible for checking the schedule and carrying out appropriate care. Marking a task complete records your entry; it does not verify that the work was performed.

6. Availability and limitations

Features depend on your browser and its storage settings. Uninterrupted availability, error-free operation, data recovery, and tree survival are not guaranteed. Keep separate records for information you cannot afford to lose. These terms do not exclude rights or protections that applicable law does not allow to be excluded.

7. Changes to this application

Features and these terms may be revised in future versions. Check this page's updated date when using a new version. A future service that adds accounts, cloud storage, or sharing should explain those changes and its data practices before you use them.

8. Questions

For questions about this installation, contact the person or organization that supplied it to you. This version does not include a support messaging service.
